# Project Background

[Back to the README](../README.md)

## Why I started

A small group can have a good time when it gets together and still lose touch between gatherings. Announcements, conversations, recommendations, and everyday updates need somewhere to live if the connection is going to continue.

I wanted a shared place for a group of men to live life together: stay informed, encourage one another, exchange ideas, and enjoy the lighter side of being part of a community.

The site brings those needs into one familiar space without turning the group into a public social network. Its purpose is practical and personal: make it easier to participate in the life of the group.

## Who it is for

The intended audience is a small, established group of men, with administrators who manage membership. The public project description focuses on friendship, shared interests, mutual support, learning, and fun.

The group's actual identity and the details of individual members are not needed to explain the project and are not included here.

## Product goals

### Keep useful information together

Provide a community information area for announcements and events so members have a shared place to look for what is happening.

### Make room for conversation

Let members post and comment, rather than limiting the site to one-way announcements. A useful community space should allow people to respond and keep a conversation going.

### Support one another

Offer a place for members to share support requests, add updates, and indicate when a request is resolved. The purpose is to help people stay aware of one another's needs, not to provide professional advice or emergency assistance.

### Share things worth discovering

Make it easy to recommend a book, an interesting article, or an inspirational or educational video. The resource library gives those recommendations a place beyond a passing conversation.

### Leave room for fun

Give the site a welcoming personality through an interactive illustrated clubhouse and seasonal details. Enjoyment is part of the purpose, not an unrelated extra.

### Work comfortably on a phone

Support the way members are likely to visit: short sessions on a mobile device. Home Screen installation and optional notifications make the site easier to return to.

## How the work developed

I led the product direction and testing, with AI assistance during development. The project grew through an iterative cycle of identifying a need, trying an implementation, testing it in use, and making adjustments.

Real-device testing helped reveal details that are easy to miss in a feature list, including how unread indicators behave when an iPhone is in the background and how photo previews work before posting.

## How I think about success

The project is useful when members can find information, participate in conversations, share worthwhile resources, and stay connected without unnecessary friction.

Those are product goals, not measured results. This documentation does not claim adoption figures, engagement increases, performance benchmarks, or other outcomes that have not been established.

## What this project is not trying to be

- A public social network or an open-membership community.
- A video-hosting platform or general-purpose file repository.
- A replacement for meeting in person.
- A confidential channel for highly sensitive information or an emergency-support service.
- A finished template that can be deployed elsewhere without review and configuration.
