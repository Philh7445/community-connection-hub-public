# Features and Current Behavior

[Back to the README](../README.md)

The features below describe the working site. Proposed additions are listed separately in the [roadmap](roadmap.md).

## Membership and administration

- Signup includes email confirmation.
- New members also require administrator approval. Confirming an email address is not the same as being approved for membership.
- The site distinguishes member and administrator roles.
- Pending membership requests appear automatically in an administrator list.
- Administrators explicitly approve or decline each request. Displaying the list does not automatically approve anyone.

## Community information

Announcements and events provide a shared place for group information and upcoming gatherings.

The goal is to help members find what is happening without relying on a single passing message.

## Conversation and comments

Members can participate in ongoing conversations through posts and comments. This supports everyday updates, questions, and discussion between gatherings.

Conversation access is membership-controlled. Photo visibility is different and is explained below.

## Member support requests

Members can share support requests, post follow-up updates, and use active or resolved status to show where things stand.

The feature is intended for ordinary community support. It is not presented as professional care, a confidential case-management system, or an emergency-response service.

## Resource library

The library provides a place to share:

- Recommended books.
- Links to inspirational or educational videos.
- Links to articles and other web resources of interest to the group.

Video sharing means sharing a link, not uploading a video file. Articles can be shared as links; the library is not a document-hosting service.

Named collections containing a manually ordered mix of books, videos, and articles are **proposed only**. They should not be shown or described as an implemented library feature.

## Interactive clubhouse

A playful illustrated clubhouse gives members objects to click and explore. Seasonal decorations help the space feel lively and familiar.

This visual personality complements the practical community features. Public documentation does not reproduce private jokes or details that identify individual members.

## Photo attachments

The photo flow includes:

1. Selecting a supported source image.
2. Previewing the attachment before posting.
3. Removing the selected image before posting if it is not wanted.
4. Acknowledging that the uploaded photo will be publicly accessible.

### Supported files

- Source formats: JPG and PNG.
- Maximum source size: 10 MB.
- Stored format: compressed JPEG.
- Maximum stored image size: 1 MB.
- Direct video uploads and general-file uploads: not supported.

Only approved members can upload photos, but stored photos are intentionally publicly accessible. These are separate controls: who can upload and who can access the resulting image.

## Mobile access

The site is mobile-friendly and can be added to a device's Home Screen.

This documentation does not claim identical installation, notification, or badge behavior across every device and browser.

## Notifications and unread indicators

- Notifications for new posts in the main conversation area are opt-in.
- Notification messages use generic content.
- A five-minute, per-device cooldown limits how often alerts are delivered.
- The unread badge has been tested on iPhone.
- The badge counts posts from other members.
- The badge can lag while the app is in the background during the cooldown.
- Viewing the relevant content clears the unread indication.

Notifications are a convenience, not a guaranteed real-time delivery channel. The cooldown is an alert-frequency limit and should not be described as a promise that every badge will refresh within five minutes.

## Current limits at a glance

- Membership control does not make uploaded photos private.
- Resource sharing uses links rather than direct video or document uploads.
- Mixed, ordered library collections are not implemented.
- Mobile notification behavior has known timing limitations.
- Hosting and storage costs are not guaranteed to stay under a fixed ceiling.
